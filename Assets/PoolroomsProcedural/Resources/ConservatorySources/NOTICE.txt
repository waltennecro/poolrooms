Author and CC0 declaration are preserved in Ground037_source.usda.txt. It is a
provenance document, not a game dependency; referenced original filenames are
identified in Ground037.json and are not claimed to be shipped beside it.

The mirrored sources are pinned at BastiaanOlij/godot_vr_climbing_demo commit
2d246e69a4da33c6a33b07fdf77ae1aec14b15db. Only these independently licensed maps
are used; no code, scenes, audio or other assets from that game are copied.

Poolrooms adaptation: decoded colour and OpenGL normal converted to PNG;
metallic/AO/detail/smoothness packed into HDRP mask. 2048x2048. No displacement
or tessellation is advertised. See tools/build_conservatory_ground.py in the
repository for deterministic preparation and Ground037.json for source hashes.

Other furniture and plant source models retain their existing attribution
under GardenSources, BistroSources, InteriorSources, MallSources and JourneySources.
Botanical meshes in ConservatoryPlants.cs are original procedural geometry,
not downloaded plant models or thousands of distinct externally acquired assets.
